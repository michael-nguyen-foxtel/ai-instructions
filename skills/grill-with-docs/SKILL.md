Run a `/grilling` session, using the `/domain-modeling` skill.

As decisions crystallise, update `CONTEXT.md` with any new or sharpened domain terms, and offer ADRs for hard-to-reverse trade-offs.

At the end, produce a final summary of the resolved design with all decisions and their rationale.
